BlogDraft Studio - HOW TO START

1. Make sure you EXTRACTED the zip (right-click the zip > Extract All). Do not run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click "BlogDraft Studio.exe". The app opens in your web browser.
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (The app is free and open source but not code-signed yet, so Windows does not know it.)
4. Follow the setup steps in the app (Setup Guide).
5. To close the app: click "Quit app" at the bottom left.

Problems? In the app click "Report a problem".
Source code: https://github.com/naeemleostar/blogdraft-studio
